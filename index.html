02 · Open source

Ster-Vis: a stereo depth camera for robots

Two low-cost cameras and a Raspberry Pi 5 that give a robot ZED-style depth, point clouds, laser scans and obstacle sectors over ROS 2 or HTTP.

  • 1.6% median depth error and 93% pixel coverage against ray-traced synthetic ground truth.
  • In Gazebo: zero collisions in two 180 s obstacle-avoidance runs, and a 20 × 12 m warehouse mapped at 2.7 cm median error.
  • Released through v2.1.0 under Apache-2.0, with CI on Linux, Windows, ARM64 and ROS 2 Jazzy.
  • Python
  • OpenCV
  • ROS 2
  • Raspberry Pi 5

03 · Ground robot

Stereo-Vision AMR

A 4WD robot that avoids obstacles using nothing but stereo depth from two Pi cameras, running Ster-Vis.

  • Chassis generated by a parametric Fusion 360 script, then 3D-printed, wired and brought up.
  • Reactive avoidance with an 8 s obstacle memory, a stale-depth watchdog that stops the motors, and a capped motor duty.
  • Drives itself on real hardware at about 6 fps depth on a Pi 5, with zero contacts in 180 s Gazebo runs.
  • Raspberry Pi 5
  • Python
  • Fusion 360
  • 3D printing
  • Gazebo
